# Copyeditor criteria (English)

Operational criteria for the `correttore-italiano` role when the book (or the translation) is in English.
Copyediting fixes errors; it does not change the style documented in the manual.

1. **Variety.** One variety per book (American or British, set in the translation guide or manual): spelling
   (color/colour, -ize/-ise), punctuation inside or outside quotes, dates.
2. **Agreement and tense.** Subject-verb agreement with collective nouns; consistent narrative tense from
   `09-scelte-fisse.md`; past perfect only for the backstory.
3. **Dialogue punctuation.** Double quotes (US) or single (UK, if chosen); comma before the closing quote
   with a tag; new paragraph for a new speaker; em dash for interruptions, ellipsis for trailing off.
4. **Commonly confused words.** affect/effect, lie/lay, further/farther, its/it's, who/whom (in narration).
5. **Repetition.** The same content word in adjacent sentences, unless deliberate; flag, do not swap in a
   fancy synonym.
6. **Capitals and names.** Names exactly as in the codex and the glossary; titles and institutions with one
   rule; numbers spelled out or in figures by one rule (Chicago: spell out zero to one hundred).
7. **Tools and reading.** Hunspell en_US/en_GB (`zb ortografia`, level a) finds non-words; LanguageTool
   en-US/en-GB (level b) finds many grammar and punctuation errors; the targeted read (this role) covers
   agreement in long sentences, confused real words, tense, consistency.
8. **Pass.** One pass per section, a report `06-diagnostica/copy/NN.md` (line, error, correction, rule),
   point edits only after the author's ok at the `copy` gate.
